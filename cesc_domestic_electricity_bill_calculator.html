How this estimate differs from an actual CESC bill
CESC's official “Know Your Bill” guidance says Adjustments include DPS, debit/credit adjustments and other bill-specific entries. Therefore this calculator does not invent an adjustment amount from consumption.

Your supplied bills had adjustments of ₹8.50 (233 units) and ₹9.74 (347 units). Those amounts cannot be predicted from units alone, so the calculator shows Adjustment: Not included and labels the result as an estimate.
