Item Price Price Is For Quantity Amount
How it works: Enter the market price, then specify exactly what quantity that price is for. For example, ₹20 for 100 gram: enter Price ₹20, Price Quantity 100, Price Unit Gram. Then enter how much you want to buy. For example, if potato is ₹40/kg and you buy 750 g, the calculator automatically calculates ₹30. If something is ₹8 per piece and you buy 6 pieces, it calculates ₹48.
